Research Statement

“TMT exposure produced a leftward shift in the alcohol dose-response curve, indicating increased interoceptive sensitivity to alcohol in males, but not females.”

- Tyler et al., 2025

Consistent with Tyler et al. (2025), I propose that exposure to traumatic stress may increase the sensitivity to alcohol. My research agenda centers on this relationship, specifically how stress-induced changes in GABA-A receptor signaling within the prelimbic cortex could modify neuronal activity and an individual’s response to alcohol. How, if in any ways, does this traumatic stress cause changes in GABA-A receptor signaling to increase alcohol activity? By examining GABA-A receptor expression and neuronal activity after stress exposure, as well as a change in this signaling can affect alcohol sensitivity, I aim to clarify whether changes in this pathway serve as the biological mechanism that connects stress exposure to sensitivity to alcohol.
